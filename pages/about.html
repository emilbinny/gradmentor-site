About GradMentor

Guidance Built on Lived Experience

GradMentor was founded by someone who has been exactly where you are — and earned the admits to prove the approach works.

Emil Binny

Principal Founding Advisor, GradMentor

GradMentor was born from a simple observation: most applicants to competitive MBA and PhD programmes are qualified — but very few know how to present that qualification compellingly. The difference between an admit and a rejection is rarely about grades or test scores. It's about story, strategy, and positioning.

Emil Mathew Binny is a funded PhD student in International Business & Strategy at Rutgers Business School, where he is also a teaching assistant for undergraduate International Business, with an MBA from Rice University (Jones Graduate School) and an MA in Applied Economics from JNU. As a Rice MBA Admissions Ambassador, he conducted 250+ candidate evaluations — seeing first-hand what separates an admit from a ding. He brings practitioner experience across corporate finance, pricing strategy, and institutional research.

GradMentor's approach is rigorous, personalised, and results-oriented — built around the client's unique story, not a templated process. Emil handles every MBA and Business PhD engagement personally; other tracks are matched to vetted advisors from a curated network.

Our Philosophy

Guide. Strategize. Succeed.

The GradMentor tagline is also our methodology. Every engagement flows through these three principles.

Guide

Every client relationship begins with listening. We invest time in understanding your background, your goals, and what makes you genuinely distinctive before we advise on a single application element.

Strategize

School selection, positioning, narrative architecture, and application timing — everything is deliberate. We approach each application as a strategic product designed for a specific audience: the admissions committee.

Succeed

Success means the admit you want, from the programme that fits your goals — ideally with funding. We don't disengage after essay submission. We're with you through interview prep, waitlists, and final decisions.
