Privacy Notice

Last updated 14 November 2026

GradMentor is an admissions advisory run by Emil Mathew Binny. This notice explains what we collect through gradmentor.org and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, optional WhatsApp number, the service you chose, your target intake and your message. The form is delivered to us by Formspree.
  • Free toolkit: your name and email, and the resources you selected. Our mailing list is run on MailerLite.
  • Call bookings: the details you enter in the calendar, which is run by Calendly.
  • Payments: made directly through your UPI app, Zelle or PayPal. We see the payer name and amount; we never see or store card or bank details.
  • WhatsApp: your number and messages if you choose to message us.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics records pages visited, buttons clicked, approximate location and the site that referred you. We do not use it to identify you personally.

How we use it

To reply to you, deliver what you asked for, run engagements you pay for, send admissions emails you signed up for, and understand which pages are useful. We do not sell your information or share your application documents with anyone outside the advisor working with you.

Your documents

Essays, transcripts, CVs and other material you send us are used only for your engagement. We never publish a client's name, result or words without written permission.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, write to hello@gradmentor.org and we will act within 30 days.
