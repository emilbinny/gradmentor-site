Questions

Before You Book

Who will I actually work with?

Emil Binny handles every MBA and business PhD engagement himself. Economics, STEM and design applicants work with the advisor from that field, named before you pay.

Do you write the SOP for me?

No. You write, we restructure, question and edit with you. Committees can tell, and ghostwritten statements fall apart at interview.

How do I pay?

UPI in rupees; Zelle or PayPal in dollars. Fixed-price services are paid in full on booking. Packages are paid 50% to start and 50% before the first submission. Payment details →

What if I only need help with one document?

Book the SOP & Essay Review. If you move to a package within 14 days, what you paid is credited.

When should I start?

For funded PhDs, about 12 to 15 months before the program begins. For MBA Round 1, by June. We still take late starters when the timeline is workable, and we will tell you on the free call if it is not.

Do you guarantee an admit?

No one honestly can. We guarantee the work: the rounds, the turnaround times and the advisor named in your quote.
